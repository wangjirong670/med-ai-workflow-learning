# Method discipline (English summary)

Full Chinese text: [`method.zh.md`](method.zh.md).

## Loop

`human dumps → AI kneads → human decides`

## Evidence discipline

1. **Traceable sources** — no unsourced external fact numbers.  
2. **Tendency whitelist** — only the track’s verifiable high grades (E1/E2/E4 on the study track). State whether you claim “matches the material” or “matches the textbook / is true.” Reconciliation proves internal consistency only.
   **Lecture notes / LLM rewrites are not E1** (cap at E2) unless they cite verifiable edition+pages checked against the real book.  
3. **`AI·待核` is not evidence** — model priors and model-only re-derivations. Exit: upgrade to a real source or delete. Never in a tendency statement.  
4. **Conflicts stay visible.**  
5. **E4 = material-verifiable operations** (counts, list↔row checks, cross-refs). “The model thought again” is not E4.

## Range slicing

In-range absence → “may continue on another page,” **not** “missing.” Later pages can overturn earlier stats via a fixed 【勘误】 block.

## Forms

| Form | Main chain |
|------|------------|
| Mechanism | mechanism chain |
| Lists / differentials | discrimination chain |
| Procedures | step chain |

No fake causality. List-like material: two-way reconciliation.

## Counter-arms

Open an arm only on a **reversal point**: in-material conflict, reconciliation mismatch, or wording fork. Otherwise one line: what was checked. Wording forks without E1 go to the upgrade list.

## Errata

Fixed block: original claim · cause · corrected · impact. No silent rewrites.

## Output skeleton

Status → structure → evidence ledger (with upgrade list) → counter-arms → human decision list. **No self-check tick lists.**
